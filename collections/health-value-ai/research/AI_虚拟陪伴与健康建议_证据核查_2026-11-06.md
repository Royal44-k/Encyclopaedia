# AI 虚拟陪伴与健康建议：原始研究定向核查

检索与核查日期：2026-11-06。本文为针对理论构建的定向证据核查，**不是系统综述**，不声称穷尽研究。证据卡用英文保留方法与统计量的原义，末尾给出中文研究含义。四篇入选原始研究包含一篇预印本、三篇正式发表论文。未把新闻报道、模型答题排行榜或相关性当作人体因果证据。未进行原始数据复算。

## 1. MIT / OpenAI：随机的是互动配置，并非使用量

Fang CM et al. *How AI and Human Behaviors Shape Psychosocial Effects of Extended Chatbot Use: A Longitudinal Randomized Controlled Study*. arXiv, v2, 2025-10-02. **Preprint**. DOI: [10.48550/arXiv.2503.17473](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.17473). [Version-specific full text and supplements](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.17473v2).

- **Design:** 2,539 enrolled; 981 completed/final sample. Four weeks; GPT-4o; randomized 3×3 modality/topic conditions, all involving AI. No non-use control. Outcomes included loneliness, human socialization, dependence, and problematic use.
- **Results:** No significant modality/topic effects on loneliness or socialization. Some nominal dependence/problematic-use findings were borderline; problematic-use pairwise differences did not survive correction (Table S12: adjusted p=.12/.089). Greater self-selected duration predicted greater dependence: b=.037, 95% CI [.016,.058], Table S15; less socialization: b=−.020, CI [−.032,−.007], Table S14. These are observational associations, not randomized dose effects.
- **Limits:** Attrition/exclusions, self-report, four-week duration, US English-speaking sample. Supplement discloses changes from preregistered analysis and exploratory mediation. Industry funding and employee coauthors disclosed. Neither harm from AI versus non-use nor safety equivalence is established.
- **Read:** Abstract; primary results; limitations; supplementary recruitment/exclusions, preregistration deviations, Tables S12–S16. For numerical CIs above, use supplementary unstandardized coefficients; main-text standardized reporting differs.

## 2. JCR：当次孤独感缓解，与持续改善需要分开

De Freitas J, Oğuz-Uğuralp Z, Uğuralp AK, Puntoni S. *AI Companions Reduce Loneliness*. Journal of Consumer Research; online 2025-06-25. DOI: [10.1093/jcr/ucaf040](https://doi.org/10.1093/jcr/ucaf040). [Author-hosted published PDF](https://www.hbs.edu/ris/Publication%20Files/AI%20Companions%20Reduce%20Loneliness%2011.7.2025_57451c02-8047-4e0d-abfc-55841f64166d.pdf).

- **Design:** Focused audit of final-paper **Study 3**, not earlier preprint numbering. 1,088 recruited, 1,072 after comprehension exclusions; three randomized groups: daily 15-minute GPT-4 companion, daily loneliness questionnaire without interaction, or one-time prediction. Seven-day completers: 314 experience, 362 control; 246 prediction participants. Attrition: 23% versus 14% in longitudinal groups, p=.001.
- **Results:** Experience-arm pooled pre/post loneliness: 36.64→30.74, d=.20, p<.001. The between-condition model comparing postinteraction ratings with control estimated b=−5.46, p=.015; four of seven daily comparisons significant. Before-interaction ratings did not differ from control across days (all p>.337). The authors explicitly conclude relief did not persist without interaction.
- **Limits:** Short horizon, differential attrition, self-report, completer primary analysis, non-time-matched control; robustness analyses reported. No evidence of lasting social recovery or replacement of human relationships. Numeric CI for the selected b was not reported in inspected main text; Figure 3 shows 95% intervals.
- **Read:** PDF pp.11–14 methods/results, pp.18–20 interpretation/limitations; Study 2 results for context. Separate web appendix not read. Data/code: [OSF](https://osf.io/hf9xe/).

## 3. Therabot：特定治疗系统可能产生健康收益

Heinz MV et al. *Randomized Trial of a Generative AI Chatbot for Mental Health Treatment*. NEJM AI. 2025;2(4). DOI: [10.1056/AIoa2400802](https://doi.org/10.1056/AIoa2400802). [Original-paper PDF mirror](https://gwern.net/doc/psychiatry/depression/2025-heinz.pdf).

- **Design:** 210 US adults with clinically significant depressive/anxiety symptoms or high eating-disorder risk; Therabot n=106 versus waitlist n=104. Four weeks of prompted use; access continued during weeks 4–8. Thus week 8 is not an off-treatment durability test.
- **Results:** At four weeks, depressive-symptom change −6.13 versus −2.63; differential d=.845, 95% CI [.409,1.282], adjusted p<.001. Anxiety d=.840 [.382,1.298], p=.001; weight concerns d=.819 [.264,1.373], p=.008. These d estimates were derived from ordinal-model log-odds, not ordinary standardized mean differences. Source: Table 1.
- **Limits:** Waitlist rather than active therapy comparison; symptom scales, short follow-up, selected participants; active suicidality/mania/psychosis excluded. Staff intervened 15 times for safety concerns and 13 times to correct inappropriate responses. This supports a supervised specialized intervention, not ordinary companion-chatbot effectiveness or equivalence to human therapists.
- **Read:** Official abstract; original PDF methods pp.3–5, Table 1 p.6, engagement/safety p.9, limitations p.12. Separate appendix not read. Trial registration: NCT06013137.

## 4. Nature Medicine：模型会答题，不代表用户会作出更好决策

Bean AM et al. *Reliability of LLMs as medical assistants for the general public: a randomized preregistered study*. Nature Medicine. 2026;32:609–615. DOI: [10.1038/s41591-025-04074-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-025-04074-y). [Full text](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-04074-y).

- **Design:** 1,298 UK adults; four randomized arms: GPT-4o, Llama 3, Command R+, or usual information sources. Up to **two scenarios per person**, sampled from ten; 600 responses per arm, 2,400 total. Data collected August–October 2024.
- **Results:** Models alone identified relevant conditions in 94.9% of cases on average; assisted users did so in fewer than 34.5%. Control participants had higher odds than pooled AI users: OR=1.76, 95% CI [1.45,2.13]. Disposition accuracy was not significantly different versus control: p=.683/.560/.861 respectively. Source: Results, “Experimental performance,” Figure 2.
- **Limits:** Hypothetical vignettes, not real morbidity outcomes; 2024 systems and UK online participants. Model-only analyses were added after preregistration. Does not show every medical AI is harmful, nor that model gains are impossible.
- **Read:** Abstract, methods, results, discussion, extended-data descriptions. Full supplementary PDF not read. [Data/code](https://github.com/am-bean/HELPMed).

**Correction checked:** [Publisher correction, 2026-04-17, DOI 10.1038/s41591-026-04404-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-026-04404-8); [Oxford repository text](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid%3A43a39bc9-ce39-4a22-99e4-12b8ec5a9a74/files/rx059c972v). Figure 2a originally mislabeled a y-axis tick as 80 instead of 40. Corrected in HTML/PDF; it does not revise the reported numerical findings.

## 对健康经济评价框架的含义（研究者推论）

不能把“AI使用时长”直接定义为健康损耗，也不能把满意、放松或亲近感直接换算成长期健康收益。建议分别记录：

1. **即时体验收益**：孤独感、痛苦或负担在一次活动前后的变化。
2. **持续功能与健康结局**：在离开该活动之后、数周或数月时是否保持改善，是否影响现实社交、工作生活功能及症状。
3. **决策质量**：用户是否理解关键信息、识别危险信号并选择适当行动；必须测量“人使用AI之后”的结果。
4. **代价与替代活动**：时间、金钱、恢复成本及被挤出的活动；替代项须依据个人处境，而不是预设户外活动必然优于虚拟活动。
5. **证据不确定性**：随机分配效果、使用量关联、机制假说应分栏报告；短期改善与长期风险可以同时存在，现有文献不足以给出统一金钱兑换率。

以上是待验证的测量设计建议，并非四篇文献已经验证了一个统一“健康价值总分”。本次没有足够证据评价AI饮食建议的长期实际代谢结局；不能由医学问答实验直接外推营养效果。
