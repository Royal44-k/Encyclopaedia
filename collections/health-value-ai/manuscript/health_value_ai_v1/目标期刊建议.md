## 本轮选择

建议把 **Social Science & Medicine 作为首个完整稿适配方向**，把 **Nature Human Behaviour Perspective 作为提案选项**，而不是同时向多个期刊正式投稿。此处不比较影响因子、收费或审稿速度，因为这些数字不是当前选刊的可靠替代标准。

Social Science & Medicine 的概念稿路线并不免除原创性要求。当前最需要解决的是框架相较健康能力、成本—后果分析、成熟个人决策指南以及健康素养型AI的不可替代增量。若只能证明“健康也重要”，无论格式多接近顶刊，学术贡献都仍不充分。

Nature Human Behaviour 的相关近期文章见 [Ivic、Ratzan与Parker的原始摘要及书目信息](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42745006/)。本轮只读取其原始摘要与元数据；没有把未取得的全文说成已精读。ScienceDirect部分作者指南及Nature页面直接抓取受限，以上要求分别来自出版商官方介绍、官方检索可见页面和学会官方稿型页。正式选刊时仍应复查完整作者指南、声明、匿名要求及稿型限制。

## 已备好的英文提案草案

**Proposed format:** Perspective

**Title:** Health economic value and intertemporal choice in the age of artificial intelligence

**Summary:** Generative AI changes task performance, access to information and the activities available to individuals. These changes need not translate directly into health or welfare gains. This Perspective connects health capital, time allocation, capability and decision science in a comparison procedure that makes feasible alternatives, total time use and intertemporal consequences explicit. It distinguishes task savings from control over time, assisted performance from retained capability, and immediate experience from persistent effects. Rather than assigning a universal monetary price to health, the proposed profile retains distinct consequences and uncertainty. Its incremental value would be tested against established decision support using common information, independent understanding and transfer outcomes, and measures of burden.

**Relevance and differentiation:** The article speaks to behavioural researchers studying work, human–AI interaction, health and choice. It explicitly acknowledges existing health-literacy and capability frameworks. Its candidate contribution is an operational connection between time accounting and comparative appraisal, coupled with a falsifiable active-control evaluation. It presents a conceptual argument and research agenda, not a completed empirical validation or a claim that technology has a uniform health effect.
