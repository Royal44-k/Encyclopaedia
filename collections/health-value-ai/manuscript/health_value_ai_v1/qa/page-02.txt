Health economic value in the AI era | Author review draft 
2 
Abstract 10 
Generative artificial intelligence changes how people produce, obtain information and spend 
time. Its value for individuals cannot be inferred from task efficiency or immediate satisfaction 
alone. We develop a conceptual framework for evaluating health-related trade-offs across work 
and everyday life, drawing on health capital, time allocation, health capability and decision 
science. The framework compares feasible alternatives over a common horizon, separates 15 
resources, health, experience and capabilities, and records uncertainty without imposing a 
universal monetary conversion. Its distinctive emphasis is the connection between task savings, 
control over time and subsequent activities, together with the distinction between assisted 
performance and independent capability. Examples from overtime compensation, food choices 
and digital companionship illustrate its boundaries. We propose evaluation against established 20 
decision support, using understanding, transfer and burden rather than a prescribed lifestyle as 
success criteria. This is a research agenda for informed choice, not a validated health valuation 
instrument. 
Keywords: health economics; artificial intelligence; health capability; time allocation; 
intertemporal choice; decision support 25 
From market rewards to personal welfare 
Consider a person offered additional pay for working late. The payment is visible, whereas the 
consequences for sleep, relationships and subsequent functioning require a different assessment. 
A larger payment can relieve material constraints while the work imposes burdens. Neither its 
size nor the presence of a health risk determines the person's overall welfare without information 30 
about alternatives, preferences and circumstances. AI-assisted work makes this comparison more 
complex: completing a task faster may change earnings, workload, discretionary time or the 
kinds of activity that remain. The relevant question is how these changes enter a person's life 
over time.
