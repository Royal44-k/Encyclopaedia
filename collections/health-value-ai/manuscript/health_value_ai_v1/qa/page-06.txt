A comparative health consequence profile 
The proposed profile begins with a feasible decision, not a health score. For an individual i, an 
option a is compared with an available alternative b over horizon T. The record contains changes 
in disposable resources, the allocation of time, physical and mental functioning, experience, and 
relevant capabilities, together with constraints and the provenance of each prediction. Figure 1 135 
summarizes these stages. Table 1 specifies a minimum record rather than an item bank that every 
study must administer. 
The first step is to make the counterfactual complete. A week of overtime followed by three 
weeks of ordinary activity cannot be compared with four weeks of overtime while ignoring the 
remaining weeks in the first option. Costs should be incremental and attributable to the option: 140 
total income is different from an overtime supplement, and a medical expenditure is different 
from a health loss. The profile should also identify effects on other people. An individual 
account and a household or societal account may require different perspectives; they should not 
be silently merged. 
The second step is to distinguish outcomes from their determinants. Sleep schedules, diet and 145 
sedentary time can be exposures or intermediate variables; symptoms, functioning and clinical 
outcomes belong to other parts of the causal account. Counting each stage as an independent 
welfare loss can duplicate the same consequence. WHO's ICF framework provides a useful 
distinction between functioning and its environmental context.[18] For this application, skin 
symptoms, vision, muscle function or metabolic measures should be included when they have a 150 
defined role in the decision, not because a larger testing panel appears more comprehensive. 
There is no universally favourable direction for every laboratory value. 
The third step is to match measures to their constructs and time scales. For example, WHO-5 
measures mental well-being over the preceding two weeks; it is not a measure of total health 
value or a diagnostic conclusion.[19] Independent task performance, perceived autonomy and 155 
immediate enjoyment require their own interpretation. A person can value an activity's pleasure 
without claiming that it improves long-term health. Equally, a short-term functional change 
should not be described as permanent damage without evidence of persistence and mechanism.
