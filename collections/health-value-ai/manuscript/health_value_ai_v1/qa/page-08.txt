Digital companionship provides a third application. Immediate relief may matter even if it does 
not persist after an interaction.[14] The relevant alternative could be another form of 
entertainment, human contact, unwanted isolation or sleep; assuming that everyone is replacing a 200 
beneficial outdoor activity prejudges the comparison. A useful record would distinguish what the 
person gains during the activity, what happens afterwards, and which activities are displaced. 
Increasing or decreasing screen time is not by itself an adequate success criterion. 
Together, these examples show why health-related choices can be economically meaningful 
without a common currency. They also delimit the proposed contribution: a profile organizes the 205 
comparison; it does not generate missing medical knowledge or establish that people have 
overlooked consequences. That latter claim requires direct evidence of omission or 
misunderstanding in the target population. 
Testing whether the framework improves decisions 
The framework needs two distinct tests. A mechanism study would investigate how access to a 210 
specified AI system changes task time, total work, control over time, activity allocation and 
subsequent outcomes. Randomizing access could identify an assignment effect in that 
environment; it would not automatically randomize actual use, organizational practice or each 
mediator. A credible analysis must distinguish the total effect of assignment from additional 
assumptions required for mediation. Recording task quality and activities outside the software 215 
prevents platform telemetry from being mistaken for a complete account of life.
