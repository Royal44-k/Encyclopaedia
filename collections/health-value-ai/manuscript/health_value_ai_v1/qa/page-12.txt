Figure 1 
 
A proposed workflow for comparative appraisal. Feasible alternatives and a common horizon 
define the comparison. Observed or predicted changes are separated into resources and time, 
health and functioning, experience, and capabilities and agency, with provenance and uncertainty 300 
retained. Personal goals and constraints enter appraisal; an explained trade-off or unresolved 
ordering can be the result. Subsequent evaluation tests understanding, transfer and burden. 
Connectors indicate the sequence of the proposed assessment, not empirically identified causal 
effects. The figure is an original schematic and contains no participant data.
