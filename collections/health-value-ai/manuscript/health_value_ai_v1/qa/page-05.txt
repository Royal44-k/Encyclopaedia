Second, assisted performance and independent capability may diverge. A classroom experiment 
comparing different generative AI tutoring arrangements showed that the design of assistance 
matters for later unaided mathematics performance.[13] The outcome concerns learning in a 
particular educational setting, not general cognitive deterioration. The implication for assessment 
is to distinguish successful performance with assistance, retained ability without it, and the 110 
person's reasons for delegating a task. Delegation can be valuable even when independent 
mastery is not the objective. 
Third, immediate experience and lasting functioning answer different questions. In the 
longitudinal component of research on AI companions, interaction relieved loneliness 
immediately, but the reported pattern did not establish persistent improvement before subsequent 115 
interactions.[14] In medical information tasks, evaluating the user matters as much as evaluating 
the model: a randomized study found that ordinary information sources outperformed pooled AI-
assisted users in identifying relevant conditions in hypothetical scenarios.[15] Neither finding 
supports a general conclusion that AI is beneficial or harmful. Earlier research on Facebook 
deactivation likewise illustrates that different welfare-relevant outcomes can move in different 120 
directions, although that study is not evidence about generative AI.[16] 
These distinctions motivate a common comparison procedure across work, information use and 
leisure. They do not imply that all digital activities share an exposure or mechanism. 
Recommendation feeds, conversational companions, clinical prediction tools and writing 
assistants should be recorded separately, with their versions, uses and alternatives. 125 
Recent work on health-literate AI already emphasizes comprehension, agency, accountability 
and proportionality.[17] This closely related argument reinforces the need to define a narrower 
contribution. The proposed profile focuses on the economic comparison of feasible activities 
over time and on whether an additional comparison module improves decisions. Supporting 
understanding or invoking agency alone would not distinguish it from existing work. 130
