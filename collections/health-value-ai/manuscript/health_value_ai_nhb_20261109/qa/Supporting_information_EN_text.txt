Extended Data Fig. 1 | A framework for comparing health-related consequences. Feasible 
alternatives and a common horizon define the comparison. Resources and time, health and functioning, 
experience, and capabilities and agency remain distinct. Priorities inform appraisal. Connections 
represent analytical steps, not estimated causal effects.

Extended Data Fig. 3 | Two research questions and their evaluation paths. Mechanism research 
explains how outcomes arise; tool evaluation tests the added comparison module. An active comparator
