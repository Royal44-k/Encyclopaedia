# Perspective editorial layout

The UI/UX Pro Max skill was queried for a research-publication design system and for reading hierarchy. Its broad design-system result included an unsuitable portfolio pattern; that pattern was rejected. A narrower style query returned the active `editorial-grid-magazine` category. The adopted guidance is editorial column structure, strong typography hierarchy, restrained accents, legible line lengths, generous figure space and consistent alignment. Web-only hover, animation, CTA and navigation recommendations were not applied to the manuscript.

The PDF uses a 522 pt content grid with two 251 pt body columns, a 20 pt gutter, 45 pt side margins, black body type and a restrained teal running label. Chinese body type is 11 pt with 16 pt leading; captions and tables use sans-serif type. Word retains native paragraphs, editable tables and equations, and embedded figures with descriptive alt text. All headers say `Perspective`.

Case pages establish the question above the illustration and then explain it in two columns. Original scenes occupy the left two thirds; precise comparison points occupy the right third. Artwork is contextual, not evidential. Monetary values and labels are added deterministically outside generated images. The three source illustrations and exact prompts are stored in `illustrations/`.

The revised edition has 14 planned pages, 11 numbered figures, 3 editable tables and 3 native display equations. The added pages separate daily pay, repeated overtime, food choice and digital leisure, preventing compressed captions or unreadably small type.
