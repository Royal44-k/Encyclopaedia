## Table 1 | What the evidence supports, and what remains to be tested

| Evidence domain | Bounded support | Inference not established | Implication for the proposed tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assisted practice and subsequent learning [E02,E05,E07] | Immediate assisted performance and later assessments can diverge in specified settings. | Long-term deterioration of existing expertise or a team-level renewal effect. | Separate assisted output, acquisition, retention and transfer under explicit tool conditions. |
| AI-supported instruction and strategy preservation [E06,E10] | Particular designs support immediate learning or preservation of strategies. | Universal superiority of AI instruction or transfer across workplace error classes. | Allow beneficial substitution and test it on common, unfamiliar cases. |
| Learning during work and assistance to tutors [E08,E09] | Outage patterns are consistent with retention; assisted tutors can improve students' lesson-level outcomes. | Randomized proof of workers' comprehensive independent capability or tutors' later unaided teaching competence. | Identify whose capability is measured and distinguish speed, quality and teaching outcomes. |
| Automation and workplace participation [E01,E03,E04] | Practice, access and participation are established concerns in expertise formation. | A generative-AI-specific effect of a particular routing policy. | Treat routing comparisons as operational tests of existing mechanisms. |
| Organizational learning and expertise renewal [E11,E12,E13,E14] | Allocation, substitution, knowledge mediation and future capability gaps already have theoretical and qualitative treatments. | A demonstrated universal expertise crisis or the full causal chain proposed here. | Acknowledge close precedents; test member–error-class distributions and specified disruptions. |
| Team cross-training, distributed expertise and external support [E15,E17,E19,E21] | Cross-training studies report shared team models and coordination-related findings; collective capability also depends on specialization and tools. | That the proposed routing-by-availability interaction has been demonstrated, or that internal coverage alone is sufficient for recovery. | Test the interaction directly and measure role-specific capability, coordination, escalation, tool access and costs separately. |
