# Unit 13 · 原创示范课内容

主题对应：人教版九年级全一册环保单元。以下脚本、短文、题目、答案及评分量规为本项目原创设计，不是教材原文或官方试题。拟用于 40 分钟综合复习，具体难度需教师试教校准。

## 学习目标

能从短报告中区分问题、行动和建议；在环保情境中运用词汇与被动表达；引用文本证据作推断；写出含具体措施与理由的环保提案。句法是复习范围建议，未宣称与用户具体版次逐页对应。

## LISTEN · The pond report

音频原稿（约 104 词，实际时长以生成文件为准）：

> Last spring, our school pond was full of plastic bottles. The water looked dark, and few birds came here. Our green team decided to make a change. We have already put two recycling bins beside the path. Every Friday, volunteers collect litter around the pond. Old wooden boxes are now used to grow flowers. However, there is still a problem. Some students leave the lights on in the greenhouse after school. We should put a reminder near the door and choose an energy monitor for each class. A clean pond is a good start, but protecting our school needs everyone's help.

任务：首听抓大意，再听抓证据；提交后才显示全文，教师可提前揭示。支持 0.85×/1× 重听，不强制倒计时。

| 题目 | 选项/操作 | 答案及解释 |
|---|---|---|
| What is the report mainly about? | A A school trip / B Improving the school environment / C Building a new library | B；报告串联池塘清理、回收与节电 |
| What has the team already done? | A Put bins beside the path / B Built a new greenhouse / C Chosen energy monitors | A；后者是建议，不是已完成行动 |
| Which problem still needs attention? | A Too many flowers / B Lights left on / C No wooden boxes | B；转折 however 后出现未解决问题 |
| What should the students do next? | 将“put up a reminder”和“choose an energy monitor”选入计划栏 | 两项均选；不能把每周捡垃圾误当作新建议 |

完成条件：前 3 题和双选均正确，允许重试；查看答案后也可完成，但标记“已查看解析”。反馈解释发生在题目旁，完成后池塘清理。

## WORDS · Useful actions

首次进入可查看词义和朗读；主任务按句子意思选择词语，不用没有语境的机械配对。资源包内仅备听力录音，单词朗读文件待正式音频阶段制作。

| 词语 | 学习说明 | 原创例句 |
|---|---|---|
| recycle | 回收加工 | We can recycle used paper. |
| reusable | 可重复使用的 | Bring a reusable bottle to school. |
| reduce | 减少 | We should reduce food waste. |
| protect | 保护 | Small actions help protect the environment. |
| litter | 垃圾；乱扔垃圾 | Please do not leave litter beside the pond. |
| turn off | 关闭 | Turn off the lights when you leave. |

四道补全：Bring a ___ bottle.（reusable）；We should ___ food waste.（reduce）；Do not leave ___ by the pond.（litter）；Please ___ the lights.（turn off）。答案花种先被选中，再点花床种下；支持拖拽但不依赖拖拽。完成四句点亮花圃，不宣称掌握全部单元词汇。

## BUILD · From waste to something useful

先把物品放到工作台，再用词块描述改造结果。句法提示展示名称与位置，不仅使用颜色。

1. 词块：Old bottles / are / used / to grow flowers / . → **Old bottles are used to grow flowers.**
2. 词块：We / have / collected / twenty cans / this week / . → **We have collected twenty cans this week.**
3. 词块：Students / should / turn off / the lights / after class / . → **Students should turn off the lights after class.**

对应解释：句 1 的主语是被使用的物品，结构为 are + used；句 2 强调本周到现在已完成的行动，have + collected；句 3 是建议，should 后接动词原形。词块范围固定，不能暗示只有这一种英语表达正确；其他自由表达由教师判断。

迁移口头题：What can old wooden boxes be used for? 示例：They can be used to grow flowers. 不自动判断发音或给出百分制口语分。

## READ · A second life for our garden

> When Green Valley School wanted a new garden corner, the students were given a small budget. At first, they planned to buy new benches and flowerpots. Then their teacher asked, “What do we already have?”
>
> The students found old wooden boxes behind the dining hall and several damaged desks in a storeroom. With help from the school worker, they repaired the wood and made two benches. The boxes were turned into flowerpots. Each pot carried a short English message about protecting the environment.
>
> Not every idea worked. The first paper signs were destroyed by rain, so the team replaced them with pieces of reused wood. They also learned that some containers needed holes at the bottom to keep plants healthy.
>
> After a month, the corner became a popular reading place. The team spent less money than expected, but its biggest success was not the money it saved. More students began to ask whether an old object could have a second life before throwing it away.

| 题目 | 答案/依据 |
|---|---|
| Why did the teacher ask what the students already had? A To delay the project / B To encourage reuse / C To sell the old desks | B；后文利用旧材料，支持这一意图推断 |
| Which problem led to a change in their plan? | 纸标牌被雨损坏，改成再用木片；必须选中第三段相应证据 |
| What does “a second life” mean here? | 让旧物有新的用途；不是物品真正复活 |
| Which statement is NOT supported? A They reused wood / B Everyone stopped buying new things / C The corner became a reading place | B；文中说更多人开始考虑再利用，没有“所有人停止购买” |
| What does the writer value most? | 思维和行为的改变；引用末段，不只回答省钱 |

界面用双栏“问题/证据”支持定位，手机改上下布局。至少完成 3 道客观题与 2 条教师/自评证据确认。主观题展示参考思路，不声称自动理解学生文本。

## ACT · Our greener-school proposal

任务：以学生环保小组身份写一份 **80–100 词**建议。包含一个观察到的问题、至少两项具体措施、理由及一项可执行的分工或检查方式。篇幅是本课建议，不是官方考试统一要求。

支架：We have noticed that… / We suggest… / …can be reused to… / This will help… / Each week, …

参考稿（仅提交后可看）：

> We have noticed that students leave plastic bottles near the playground and keep some classroom lights on after school. Our green team suggests putting recycling bins beside the main paths. We can also make reminder signs from used wood instead of buying new materials. Each class should choose an energy monitor to check the lights before everyone leaves. These actions will reduce waste and save energy. Every Friday, volunteers can record the problems they find and share their results. We hope more students will join us and help make our school greener.

人工评价量规（每项 0–2 分，共 8 分）：内容完整；措施具体且可执行；语言表达清楚且语法基本准确；组织连贯。字数自动统计只是写作辅助，不能代替内容和语言评价。教师投影时可小组口头汇报后确认完成；个人模式标记自评完成，不标“自动批改通过”。

## 退出卡与数据

三句反思：One problem I noticed… / One action I can take… / One expression I can use… 。记录本保存站点完成情况、错误重试、是否揭示答案、写作草稿和自评。姓名非必填；无录音上传、无自动远程识别、无假排名。
